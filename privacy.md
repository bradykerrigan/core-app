# Core privacy policy

Core is a personal desktop assistant written by and for one person, Brady Kerrigan. It is not offered to anyone else.

When Core connects to a Google account, it reads Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive on that one account, on the owner's own computer, to prepare a daily briefing and to carry out tasks the owner asks for. Everything it reads stays on that computer. Nothing is sent to any third party, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's security settings (Third-party apps with account access). Core keeps no copy of Google data outside the owner's computer.
